# Glass that behaves

A calmer, faster Mine Music: real transitions, lyrics that move with the voice, a
whole library you can edit, and an app that picks up where you left it.

## New
- **Equalizer** — 23 presets, pre-amp with clipping protection, loudness and bass, hold-to-compare, saved presets and per-output profiles
- **Song transitions** — crossfade and AutoMix (beat-matched, tempo-stretched, loudness-balanced), drawn inside the seek bar
- **Kinetic lyrics** — word/syllable sweep, feathered edges, held-word swell, translation and romanization
- **Software update** — signed APK download with resume and SHA-256 verification, plus the release notes you are reading
- **Library you can edit** — Liked Songs, Your Playlists, History, Downloaded, Local Files and YouTube Music playlists, with search, sort, filters, swipe and multi-select
- **Spotify import** — paste any public playlist link, no account needed
- **Pure black (AMOLED)** — true-black shell, off by default
- **Song Transitions + font and weight** settings

## Improved
- **Album, artist and playlist pages** — one full-bleed surface that dissolves into the page, liquid-glass chrome, live sort
- **Home** — one shelf per job, quick picks ranked from what you actually play, per-shelf card or list layout
- **Touch** — every pill, circle, chip and toggle compresses under the finger and springs back; one haptic per touch
- **Performance** — 120 Hz, baked blurs instead of live full-screen filters, narrower rebuilds
- **Downloads** — parallel ranged transfer with resume and cache reuse

## Fixed
- The app no longer forgets your queue, song or position when Android closes it
- Equalizer presets overflowed the screen on narrow phones
- Settings no longer flashes black or drops frames while scrolling
- Deleting a playing playlist no longer stops playback
- A local file with an apostrophe in its name no longer crashes the scanner
- Back closes the page you are on instead of the whole app
